Review of Broom Hair & Harbour Barber (Ullapool) — Check Winter vs Summer Hours!

Post #1 by zoe_munro1922 (Zoe Munro, Ullapool) • 2026-11-11 17:22 GMT

Visited Ullapool this month hoping to try Gents Clipper & Scissor Cut (£17.00). Watch out if your cottage welcome folder has the old Summer 2026 leaflet (Tue-Sat 09:00-18:00 (Thu late 19:30)) — since 26 Oct they are on Winter GMT hours: Tue-Sat 09:30-16:30; Sun-Mon Closed. Once we went back at the right time, top quality!
