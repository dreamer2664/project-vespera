Review of Lochbroom Pharmacy & Dispensing Chemist (Ullapool) — Check Winter vs Summer Hours!

Post #1 by kevin_carmichael1924 (Kevin Carmichael, Visiting from Hamburg) • 2026-11-11 00:58 GMT

Visited Ullapool this month hoping to try Loperamide 2mg Capsules (6 pack) (£3.49). Watch out if your cottage welcome folder has the old Summer 2026 leaflet (Mon-Fri 09:00-18:00; Sat 09:00-16:00) — since 26 Oct they are on Winter GMT hours: Mon-Fri 09:00-17:30 (Closed 13:00-14:00 dispensing break); Sat 09:00-13:00; Sun Closed. Once we went back at the right time, top quality!

Owner Response (Dr. Sorcha Boyle, MPharm): Thanks for calling in! Yes, all Wester Ross shops switch from summer hours on the Monday after BST ends (26 Oct). Glad you enjoyed it.
