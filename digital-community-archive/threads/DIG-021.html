Review of Rhue Art & Lighthouse Studio Gallery (Ullapool) — Check Winter vs Summer Hours!

Post #1 by kirsteen_ross1944 (Kirsteen Ross, Visiting from Hamburg) • 2026-11-05 22:40 GMT

Visited Ullapool this month hoping to try Framed Linocut 'Stac Pollaidh in Spate' (£240.00). Watch out if your cottage welcome folder has the old Summer 2026 leaflet (Tue-Sun 10:30-17:30) — since 26 Oct they are on Winter GMT hours: Fri-Sun 11:00-16:00; Mon-Thu Closed. Once we went back at the right time, top quality!
