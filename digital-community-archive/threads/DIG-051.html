Review of Summer Isles Sea Tours — MV Shearwater (Ullapool) — Check Winter vs Summer Hours!

Post #1 by niamh_abercrombie1948 (Niamh Abercrombie, Polbain) • 2026-11-03 20:10 GMT

Visited Ullapool this month hoping to try Adult 4-Hour Tanera & Seal Cruise (£48.00). Watch out if your cottage welcome folder has the old Summer 2026 leaflet (Daily Sailings 10:00 & 14:15 (01 Apr - 18 Oct)) — since 26 Oct they are on Winter GMT hours: CLOSED FOR WINTER REFIT (19 Oct 2026 - 31 Mar 2027). Once we went back at the right time, top quality!
