Review of Ullapool Print, Sign & Copy Shop (Ullapool) — Check Winter vs Summer Hours!

Post #1 by kevin_mackay1972 (Kevin Mackay, Ullapool) • 2026-11-13 08:04 GMT

Visited Ullapool this month hoping to try 100x A4 120gsm Archival Bond Sheets (£18.00). Watch out if your cottage welcome folder has the old Summer 2026 leaflet (Mon-Fri 09:00-17:30; Sat 09:30-12:30) — since 26 Oct they are on Winter GMT hours: Mon-Fri 09:00-16:30; Sat-Sun Closed. Once we went back at the right time, top quality!
