BS 341 Valve Outlet & Regional Cylinder Delivery Standards

All compressed industrial and specialty isotopic calibration gases supplied from our Longman Inverness depot conform to BS 341. Customers are reminded that flammable and toxic reference cylinders—including Carbon Monoxide (BS 341 No. 4, 19.0 mm A/F grooved brass nut) and Hydrogen/Methane mixtures—employ LEFT-HAND (counter-clockwise tightening) bullnose threads, whereas inert Argon, Nitrogen, Helium, and Krypton cylinders employ standard right-hand threads.
