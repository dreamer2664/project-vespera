Welcome to Achnahaird Pottery & Sea-Glaze Kiln

Achnahaird Pottery & Sea-Glaze Kiln has served residents, crofters, mariners, and visitors in Achnahaird and the wider Lochbroom & Coigach district since 2013. Located at 2 Achnahaird Steading, IV26 2YW, we operate year-round with dedicated seasonal schedules adapted to Wester Ross daylight and weather conditions.
