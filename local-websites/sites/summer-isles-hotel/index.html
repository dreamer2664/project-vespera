Welcome to The Summer Isles Hotel & Dining Room

The Summer Isles Hotel & Dining Room has served residents, crofters, mariners, and visitors in Achiltibuie and the wider Lochbroom & Coigach district since 1967. Located at Main Brae, Achiltibuie, IV26 2YG, we operate year-round with dedicated seasonal schedules adapted to Wester Ross daylight and weather conditions.
