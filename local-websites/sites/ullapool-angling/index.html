Welcome to Ullapool Sea Angling & Skate Charter (MV Northern Star)

Ullapool Sea Angling & Skate Charter (MV Northern Star) has served residents, crofters, mariners, and visitors in Ullapool and the wider Lochbroom & Coigach district since 1999. Located at Pontoon C, Ullapool Harbour, IV26 2UH, we operate year-round with dedicated seasonal schedules adapted to Wester Ross daylight and weather conditions.
