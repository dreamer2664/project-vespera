Welcome to Seaforth Chippy & Seafood Bar

Seaforth Chippy & Seafood Bar has served residents, crofters, mariners, and visitors in Ullapool and the wider Lochbroom & Coigach district since 1979. Located at 6 Quay Street, Ullapool, IV26 2UE, we operate year-round with dedicated seasonal schedules adapted to Wester Ross daylight and weather conditions.
