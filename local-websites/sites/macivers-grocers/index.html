Welcome to MacIver's Harbour Grocers & Provisions

MacIver's Harbour Grocers & Provisions has served residents, crofters, mariners, and visitors in Ullapool and the wider Lochbroom & Coigach district since 1962. Located at 9-11 Argyle Street, Ullapool, IV26 2UB, we operate year-round with dedicated seasonal schedules adapted to Wester Ross daylight and weather conditions.
