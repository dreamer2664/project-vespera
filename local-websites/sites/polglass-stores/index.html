Welcome to Polglass General Stores & Post Office

Polglass General Stores & Post Office has served residents, crofters, mariners, and visitors in Achiltibuie and the wider Lochbroom & Coigach district since 1954. Located at Polglass Road, Achiltibuie, IV26 2YG, we operate year-round with dedicated seasonal schedules adapted to Wester Ross daylight and weather conditions.
