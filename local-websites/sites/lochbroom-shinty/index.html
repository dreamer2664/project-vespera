Welcome to Lochbroom Camanachd (Shinty Club)

Lochbroom Camanachd (Shinty Club) has served residents, crofters, mariners, and visitors in Ullapool and the wider Lochbroom & Coigach district since 1928. Located at Morefield Pitch, Ullapool, IV26 2SR, we operate year-round with dedicated seasonal schedules adapted to Wester Ross daylight and weather conditions.
