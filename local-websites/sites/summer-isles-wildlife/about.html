About Summer Isles Wildlife & Seabird Trust (Founded 2003)

Established in 2003 and currently led by Warden: Dr. Isla MacKinnon, Summer Isles Wildlife & Seabird Trust forms part of the working commercial and civic fabric connecting Ullapool, Achiltibuie, Altandhu, and the mainland causeway shore at Polbain.

Premises & Local Supply Chain

Our premises at Old Pier Cottage, Polbain, IV26 2YW rely on road freight along the A835 over the Dirrie More from Inverness and, for Coigach peninsula customers, the single-track C1047 coast road west of Drumrunie Junction. During autumn and winter equinoctial gales, timings are coordinated with Highland Council winter maintenance, Ullapool Harbour Trust tide advisories, and Westerbus community transport.

Archival Transparency

All tariff revisions and seasonal timetable switches (when British Summer Time ends on the final Sunday of October) are published on our Opening Hours and Notices pages, as well as in The Wester Ross Daily Mercury and The Coigach & Minch Gazette.
