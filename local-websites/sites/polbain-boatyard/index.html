Welcome to Polbain Boatyard & Outboard Slip

Polbain Boatyard & Outboard Slip has served residents, crofters, mariners, and visitors in Polbain and the wider Lochbroom & Coigach district since 1990. Located at Old Quay Road, Polbain, IV26 2YW, we operate year-round with dedicated seasonal schedules adapted to Wester Ross daylight and weather conditions.
