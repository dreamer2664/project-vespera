Welcome to The Arch Inn & Bistro

The Arch Inn & Bistro has served residents, crofters, mariners, and visitors in Ullapool and the wider Lochbroom & Coigach district since 1840. Located at 10 West Shore Street, Ullapool, IV26 2UR, we operate year-round with dedicated seasonal schedules adapted to Wester Ross daylight and weather conditions.
