Welcome to Highland Council — Wester Ross Service Point & Library

Highland Council — Wester Ross Service Point & Library has served residents, crofters, mariners, and visitors in Ullapool and the wider Lochbroom & Coigach district since 1996. Located at Mill Street, Ullapool, IV26 2UN, we operate year-round with dedicated seasonal schedules adapted to Wester Ross daylight and weather conditions.
