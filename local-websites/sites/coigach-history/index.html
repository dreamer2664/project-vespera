Welcome to Coigach Historical & Genealogy Society

Coigach Historical & Genealogy Society has served residents, crofters, mariners, and visitors in Achiltibuie and the wider Lochbroom & Coigach district since 1988. Located at Reading Room, Coigach Hall, IV26 2YG, we operate year-round with dedicated seasonal schedules adapted to Wester Ross daylight and weather conditions.
