Frequently Asked Questions — Local Access & Conditions

1. Why do printed flyers in holiday cottages sometimes show later closing hours?

Many self-catering folders in Polbain, Achiltibuie, and Ullapool still contain Summer 2026 or 2025 print runs. Always check the effective date range on the leaflet footer against our Winter 2026–27 Schedule (effective 26 October 2026).

2. How do gales and spring tides affect Coigach & Polbain deliveries?

Single-track road C1047 is exposed to crosswinds west of Drumrunie, and low-lying shore aprons at Polbain Pier and the adjacent private Eilean Vespera tidal causeway (An Clachan Dubh) take heavy breaking spray whenever WNW storm surges coincide with high water.

3. Do card terminals work if rural phone or power lines blink?

Mainland Ullapool sits on the Lochbroom primary ring, whereas rural Coigach properties on the 11kV overhead spur occasionally experience brief auto-reclose dips in 55+ knot squalls. Carrying a small float of cash in winter is sensible.
