Welcome to The Ferry Boat Inn

The Ferry Boat Inn has served residents, crofters, mariners, and visitors in Ullapool and the wider Lochbroom & Coigach district since 1892. Located at 22 Shore Street, Ullapool, IV26 2UJ, we operate year-round with dedicated seasonal schedules adapted to Wester Ross daylight and weather conditions.
