Welcome to SSEN North Highland Network & Outage Portal

SSEN North Highland Network & Outage Portal has served residents, crofters, mariners, and visitors in Inverness and the wider Lochbroom & Coigach district since 1998. Located at Henderson Road, Inverness, IV1 1SN, we operate year-round with dedicated seasonal schedules adapted to Wester Ross daylight and weather conditions.
