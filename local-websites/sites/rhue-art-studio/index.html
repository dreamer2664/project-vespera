Welcome to Rhue Art & Lighthouse Studio Gallery

Rhue Art & Lighthouse Studio Gallery has served residents, crofters, mariners, and visitors in Ullapool and the wider Lochbroom & Coigach district since 1996. Located at Rhue Point Road, Ullapool, IV26 2TJ, we operate year-round with dedicated seasonal schedules adapted to Wester Ross daylight and weather conditions.
