Welcome to Quayside Fishmongers & Ice Co.

Quayside Fishmongers & Ice Co. has served residents, crofters, mariners, and visitors in Ullapool and the wider Lochbroom & Coigach district since 1988. Located at Unit 2 Fishmarket Pier, Ullapool, IV26 2UH, we operate year-round with dedicated seasonal schedules adapted to Wester Ross daylight and weather conditions.
