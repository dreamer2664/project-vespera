Welcome to Hebridean Sea-Salt & Kelp Co.

Hebridean Sea-Salt & Kelp Co. has served residents, crofters, mariners, and visitors in Polbain and the wider Lochbroom & Coigach district since 2020. Located at Unit 1 Pier Store Yard, Polbain, IV26 2YW, we operate year-round with dedicated seasonal schedules adapted to Wester Ross daylight and weather conditions.
