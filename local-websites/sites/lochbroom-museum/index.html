Welcome to Lochbroom Museum & Heritage Society

Lochbroom Museum & Heritage Society has served residents, crofters, mariners, and visitors in Ullapool and the wider Lochbroom & Coigach district since 1991. Located at Former Telford Church, West Argyle St, Ullapool, IV26 2TY, we operate year-round with dedicated seasonal schedules adapted to Wester Ross daylight and weather conditions.
