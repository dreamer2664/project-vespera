Welcome to Lochbroom Sailing, Coastal Rowing & Skiff Club

Lochbroom Sailing, Coastal Rowing & Skiff Club has served residents, crofters, mariners, and visitors in Ullapool and the wider Lochbroom & Coigach district since 1972. Located at West Shore Boatshed, Ullapool, IV26 2UR, we operate year-round with dedicated seasonal schedules adapted to Wester Ross daylight and weather conditions.
