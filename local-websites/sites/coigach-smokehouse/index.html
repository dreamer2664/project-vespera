Welcome to The Coigach Smokehouse & Pier Larder

The Coigach Smokehouse & Pier Larder has served residents, crofters, mariners, and visitors in Polbain and the wider Lochbroom & Coigach district since 1998. Located at 4 Old Quay Road, Polbain, IV26 2YW, we operate year-round with dedicated seasonal schedules adapted to Wester Ross daylight and weather conditions.
