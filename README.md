# Project Vespera — Documentary & Regional Archive

**Archive Scope:** Eilean Vespera, Coigach Peninsula (Polbain / Achiltibuie), and Lochbroom / Ullapool District, Wester Ross, Scotland (`November 2026`).

This repository houses the public documentary, institutional, municipal, commercial, maritime, broadcast, cartographic, photographic, and digital community records assembled around **Case Reference MIT-2026-1114-V** (*Eilean Vespera Observatory, 14–15 November 2026*).

## Directory Overview

- `VERSION_1_ARCHIVE/` — Preserved baseline evidentiary dossier (`EX-01` through `EX-48`, baseline maps, scene plates, and initial 66-item schedule).
- `expanded-case-archive/` — Supplementary forensic, chain-of-custody, and procedural case bundles.
- `interviews/` — Field and street interview transcripts collected across Polbain, Achiltibuie, Ullapool, and surrounding townships.
- `business-print-archive/` — Commercial pamphlets, menus, flyers, notices, and printed schedules from local enterprises.
- `local-websites/` — Offline-navigable HTML websites and dated web captures from local businesses, public bodies, clubs, and services (`local-websites/index.html`).
- `newspapers/` — Complete consecutive daily editions of regional and community newspapers (`10–16 November 2026`).
- `photographic-archive/` — Evidentiary scene plates, locality documentation photographs, contact sheets, and thumbnails.
- `maps-and-surveys/` — Street maps, nautical charts, harbour layouts, architectural plans, engineering schematics, and survey diagrams.
- `radio-archive/` — Local broadcast transcripts, maritime safety bulletins, and station transmission logs.
- `digital-community-archive/` — Offline captures of regional forums, community noticeboards, blogs, classifieds, and review portals.
- `public-notices/` — Municipal, harbour, transport, weather, and community posters and announcements.
- `transport-and-maritime/` — Ferry, bus, taxi, freight, harbourmaster, vehicle maintenance, and causeway logs.
- `municipal-and-utility-records/` — Council minutes, planning warrants, water, electrical network, and public institution files.
- `institute-administration/` — Operational, HR, safety, facilities, procurement, and archival records of the Vespera Institute and regional partners.
- `correspondence/` — Personal and commercial letters, emails, circulars, receipts, and diary extracts.
- `raw-data/` — Structured sensor exports, meteorological/tidal logs, utility telemetry, and operational CSV/JSON datasets.
- `magazines-and-newsletters/` — Issues of local newsletters, club bulletins, heritage journals, and trade circulars.
- `event-programmes/` — Dated festival packs, theatre schedules, market programmes, and fixture calendars.
- `catalogues-and-pricelists/` — Commercial product catalogues, chandlery lists, laboratory supply sheets, and trade tariffs.
- `historical-archive/` — Lighthouse board records, oral histories, parish directories, and heritage documentation.
- `questionnaires/` — Expanded comprehensive objective investigation questionnaire.
- `assets-and-sources/` — Vector source files (`SVG`) and shared archival reference standards.
- `indexes/` — Neutral file, exhibit, publication, and directory catalogues.

*All materials in this archive are self-contained and designed for offline inspection.*
