# Project Vespera — Documentary & Regional Archive

**Archive Scope:** Eilean Vespera, Coigach Peninsula (Polbain / Achiltibuie), and Lochbroom / Ullapool District, Wester Ross, Scotland (November 2026).

This repository houses the public documentary, institutional, municipal, commercial, maritime, broadcast, cartographic, photographic, and digital community records assembled around **Case Reference MIT-2026-1114-V** — the investigation into the death of Dr. Alistair Vance at the Eilean Vespera Observatory on the night of 14–15 November 2026.

*All materials in this archive are fictional, self-contained and designed for offline inspection. No external resources are required.*

## Reading Notes

- **Times:** all timestamps are UTC (= GMT). British Summer Time ended on 25 October 2026.
- **Weather window:** severe gale (Storm Fergus), 14–15 November 2026; coastal surge submerged the tidal causeway and isolated Eilean Vespera.
- **Versioning:** many business hours are printed in multiple versions (legacy 2025, Summer 2026 BST, Autumn/Winter 2026 GMT, and dated storm-alteration notices). Always check which version is authoritative for a given date.
- **Photographs:** plates marked *scene* depict the documented scene; plates marked *reconstruction* depict post-investigation reconstructions. No plate depicts graphic injury.
- **Neutrality:** nothing in this archive is labelled as more or less important than anything else. Weight the evidence yourself.
