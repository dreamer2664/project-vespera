OPERATION VESPERA — Documentary & Regional Archive

Coigach · Lochbroom · Eilean Vespera · Wester Ross, Scotland · All times UTC (GMT)
This archive assembles the public record surrounding the investigation into the death of Dr. Alistair Vance. It contains baseline exhibits, supplemental case schedules, photography and forensic plates, charts and plans, complete runs of local newspapers and broadcasts, a street-canvass of the district, business print materials, offline websites, public notices, transport and utility telemetry, institutional filings, correspondence and machine-readable datasets. Everything here is fictional and self-contained; all times are UTC (GMT). Nothing is labelled or weighted — assess it yourself. Start with the questionnaire or the master file index.
